# QuietEarth

## The Newsletter That Outlasts Everything

QuietEarth is the PyrateRadio newsletter for analog radio knowledge, band conditions notes, simple P2P setups, and occasional observations from the Gulf and Caribbean monitoring perspective.

### The Hook

This newsletter only stops when there is a major EMP event, a civilization-ending natural disaster, or a GitHub token that was compromised by a lowly human being who is keeping the newsletter alive in his 102nd year of existence in Hell — well after AI overlords and K-Pop hate-fueled North Korean super soldiers have long been dead.

Until then, it keeps going.

### What You Get

- Practical notes on analog systems that still work
- Occasional band activity observations
- Simple equipment suggestions for friends and family
- No hype. No crypto. Just radios.
