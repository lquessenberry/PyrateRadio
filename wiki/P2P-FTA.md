# P2P / FTA Basics

Peer-to-peer (P2P) and free-to-air (FTA) analog systems are the ones that work radio-to-radio with the lowest barriers.

## Highest Leverage for Most People

### Legal CB (11 m / 27 MHz)
- No license
- 4 W AM / 12 W SSB
- Widely available radios and antennas
- Local simplex + occasional skip
- SSB preferred for better range; upper channels less crowded

### GMRS / FRS (UHF)
- FRS: fully license-free, common bubble-pack handhelds
- GMRS: inexpensive family license, higher power, same channels
- Excellent for neighborhood and property-level coordination

### Passive Monitoring

A basic shortwave or HF receiver covering the lower marine and amateur segments lets you hear real traffic (weather, vessel activity, emergency nets) without transmitting.

## Simple Teaching Setup

1. A couple of legal CB radios (one SSB-capable if possible) with basic antennas
2. A pair of FRS/GMRS handhelds
3. A receiver that covers 2–8 MHz marine USB plus 80 m and 40 m

## Core Message

When digital systems fail, these still talk radio-to-radio. Local analog is the reliable capability. Skip is the variable bonus. No keys, no servers, no infrastructure required beyond power and an antenna.
