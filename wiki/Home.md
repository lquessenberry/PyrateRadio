# Home

Welcome to PyrateRadio — a practical reference for analog signals intelligence (SIGINT) and resilient communications.

## What This Covers

- License-free and low-barrier analog systems (CB, FRS/GMRS)
- HF bands that remain useful when higher infrastructure fails
- Monitoring techniques for regional awareness (Gulf Coast, Caribbean, and beyond)
- Peer-to-peer voice that works without towers, internet, or digital protocols

## Core Idea

Digital systems fail. Analog radios that talk directly to other radios do not depend on the same infrastructure. Local ground-wave is the reliable baseline. Skywave (skip) is the occasional bonus.
